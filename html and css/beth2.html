Bethlehem Kudumba Unit

2026 2h 37m

Malayalam Comedy Drama Romance

Synopsis

After years apart pursuing their respective careers, childhood sweethearts Ashley and Justin find themselves unexpectedly reunited when they both return to their quaint hometown for a massive, chaotic family gathering—the Bethlehem Kudumba Unit's annual festival. Amidst meddling relatives, rekindled inside jokes, and the unavoidable nostalgia of their shared past, they must navigate the delicate balance of their current independent lives and the undeniable spark that still exists between them. This heartwarming tale explores the complexities of modern love rooted in deep traditions, blending humor and romance in a beautifully sunlit setting.
